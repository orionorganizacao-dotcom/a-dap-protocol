# Credit Scoring

## Problem

AI systems approve or reject financial credit.

Traditional systems:

Decision
↓
Action
↓
Log
↓
Explanation

Problem:

Logs can be modified.

Explanations can be generated after financial outcomes become known.

Retrospective narratives are not equivalent to independent evidence.

## A-DAP Flow

Decision
↓
Envelope Creation
↓
Hash Generation
↓
Independent Verification
↓
Action

## Benefit

Provides independently reconstructable evidence that a credit decision existed before later outcomes.
