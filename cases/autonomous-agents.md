# Autonomous Agents

## Problem

Agentic AI systems perform actions without continuous human supervision.

Traditional systems:

Decision
↓
Action
↓
Log
↓
Explanation

Problem:

Logs can be modified.

Explanations may be generated after execution.

Retrospective narratives are not equivalent to independent evidence.

## A-DAP Flow

Decision
↓
Envelope Creation
↓
Hash Generation
↓
Independent Verification
↓
Action

## Benefit

Provides verifiable evidence that an autonomous agent decision existed before execution.
